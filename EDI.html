Equity, Diversity, Inclusion

Directed Reading Program (DRP)

I have participated in Directed Reading Programs as a mentee and mentor. Assuming that impediments to the exercise of mathematical ability are largely socially constructed, I think DRPs can be a powerful tool for democratizing access to math research. DRPs naturally select for those who are curious about mathematics but are uncertain about what deeper exploration might entail. After the first DRP at the University of Waterloo, I wrote down my perspective on the purpose and possible structures for DRPs for future generations of mentors: DRP Mentor Guide. The WiM chapter at the University of Waterloo has far exceeded the scope of the guide above, and I highly encourage reaching out to them for advice. The guide above is simply inspiration for what can be done with relatively few tools.
